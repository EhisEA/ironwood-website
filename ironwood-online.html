RealRespondent™

Real respondents. Defended in layers.

Ironwood’s first-line countermeasures integrate SI-powered technology and scoring algorithms, backed by human review.

Technology & SI countermeasures
  • Captcha visual testing to separate humans from bots
  • Verisoul verification: device fingerprinting, real-time geolocation, fake-email detection and behavioral signals
  • Proprietary digital fingerprinting: IP detection, duplication and geolocation
  • Pre-set accuracy thresholds to flag speeders, straightliners and cheaters
  • Visual and open-end traps
Human-led countermeasures
  • Design and analysis of “red herring” questions
  • Analysis of open-ends for sensibility and accuracy
  • Micro-attention tests, such as instructional manipulation checks
  • Macro attention thresholds that catch straight-lining and speeding
