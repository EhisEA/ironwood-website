Capabilities

The Ironwood Co-Intelligence™ SI Solution Suite.

Online fraud detection

RealRespondent™ uses a rigorous, multi-layered process: SI-powered technology and scoring algorithms detect and prevent malicious activity in real time during the screener and survey, with human logic and analysis integrated to ensure authenticity.

Synthetic respondents

With PersonaPanels™, our synthetic respondents are modeled and validated to mirror the behaviors and traits of real human segments, from proprietary audiences to generations like Gen Z or Boomers.

ProbeSI™ interviewing tools

Qualitative: SI can simulate moderator interactions in early-stage interviews and asynchronous discussions. Quantitative: chatbots probe and clarify open-ends and help detect bots and fraudsters.
